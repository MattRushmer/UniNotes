What both titles cover (general knowledge):
Standard finite mathematics for business/social sciences:
- Linear functions, equations, and models
- Systems of equations and matrices (Gauss-Jordan, inverses)
- Linear programming (graphical and simplex methods)
- Mathematics of finance (interest, annuities, amortization)
- Sets, counting, and probability (incl. conditional probability, Bayes)
- Statistics: describing data, distributions, and inference
- Markov chains and game theory

Why it matches this course:
MATH503 Weeks 3-6 lecture topics align with the algebra, probability,
and finance sections of these texts.
