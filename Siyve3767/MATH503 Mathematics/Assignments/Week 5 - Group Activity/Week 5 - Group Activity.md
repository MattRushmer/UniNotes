# Week 5 - Group Activity (Old Faithful)

> In-pair activity based on the [[MATH503 Mathematics/Assignments/Week 5 - Group Activity/MATH503 Group Task for Week 5_2026.docx|task brief]] and the [[MATH503 Mathematics/Assignments/Week 5 - Group Activity/Old faithful dataset.xlsx|Old faithful dataset]].

## What it is
- Work in pairs; one submission per pair
- Data: waiting time (in minutes) between eruptions of the Old Faithful geyser
- Submit a Word file named `MATH503_Week5_ID1_ID2.docx` via online submission

## Dataset
- [[MATH503 Mathematics/Assignments/Week 5 - Group Activity/Old faithful dataset.xlsx|Old faithful dataset.xlsx]] — Sheet1 holds **16 rows ("days") × 18 columns** of waiting times in minutes (values ~43–108). Sheet2 is empty.
- Each group is given one "day" (row); Stage 3 uses a second day.

## Stage 1 — initial prediction
- Record each student's guess for the next eruption's waiting time, with no data.

## Stage 2 — estimate from one day
- Use your assigned day's data to estimate the waiting time (graph and/or summary statistics).
- Record: which day, your estimate, copies of any graphs, and your justification.

## Stage 3 — compare with a second day
- Investigate a second day's data and make a new prediction.
- Record: comparison of the two days (summary stats + graphs), updated estimate, graphs, justification, and two sentences on an insight you found.

## Stage 4 — share with another group
- Compare with a group that used different data.
- Record: their reasoning, your commentary on their graphs, and what their graphs revealed.
- Required question: what do **stem-and-leaf**, **box-and-whisker**, **histogram**, and **time-series** plots reveal about a dataset's distribution?
