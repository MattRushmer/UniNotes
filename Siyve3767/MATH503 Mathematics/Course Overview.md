# MATH503 — Course Overview

> Summarised from [[MATH503 Mathematics/MATH503Combined Manual_2026_S2_2.pdf|the course manual]] and [[MATH503 Mathematics/MATH503 Course Content Schedule-1.docx|the content schedule]].

## Content by week
- **Week 1** — Probability and Sets
- **Week 2** — Probability and Sets; Venn Diagrams
- **Week 3** — Conditional Probability; Permutations
- **Week 4** — Combinations; Probability Applications
- **Week 5** — Statistical Measures
- **Week 6** — Probability Distributions
- **Week 7** — Probability Distributions; Hypothesis Testing
- **Week 8** — Hypothesis Testing; Relations
- **Week 9** — Relations and Functions
- **Week 10** — Matrices
- **Week 11** — Matrices
- **Week 12** — Review

## Assessment
- **Portfolio — 30%** — group tasks in tutorials and quizzes (ongoing)
- **Assignment — 25%** — Myimaths online activities (ongoing)
- **Final Controlled Assessment — 45%** — 3 hours, Week 13–15 (date TBA)
- All assessments must be attempted; pass = **50% overall**

## Other course info
- **Aim**: introduce the mathematical and statistical concepts needed for analysing data and for computing applications
- **Content**: sets, probability, probability distributions, statistical measures, functions, relations, matrix operations
- **Textbooks**: Lial et al. *Finite Mathematics* [LGR]; Tan *Finite Mathematics for the managerial, life, and social sciences* [T]
- **Myimaths**: www.myimaths.com for the 25% assignment (personal login by Week 2)
- Two-hour face-to-face lecture + tutorial (part of the credit comes from tutorial group work and quizzes)
