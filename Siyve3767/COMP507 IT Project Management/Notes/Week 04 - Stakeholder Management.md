# Week 04 - Stakeholder Management

> Auto-extracted from [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Lectures/Week 04 - Stakeholder Management.pptx|Week 04 - Stakeholder Management.pptx]] — Daniel Vaipulu

## Why it's a knowledge area
- Stakeholder management became the **10th knowledge area** in PMBOK 5th ed (2012)
- Purpose: identify all stakeholders, learn their expectations, find the critical ones, engage effectively

## Identify stakeholders
- **Stakeholder register**: name, position, type/category, project role, contact details — comprehensive template
- Two categories: **internal** (within the org, incl. team + passive stakeholders) and **external** (suppliers, consultants, contractors)
- **Stakeholder analysis**: learn expectations, identify critical stakeholders, complete a **power–interest grid**
- **Engagement levels**: unaware · resistant · neutral · supportive · leading

## Plan & manage stakeholder engagement
- **Stakeholder management plan**: management strategies per stakeholder, communication requirements
- **Expectations management matrix**: ranks scope/time/cost goals as the sponsor prioritises them
- **Issue management**: issue log (document, monitor, track); defined procedures (capture → assign → resolution timeframe → monitor → close); 5-working-day rule in this course; **unresolved issues become risks** and impact scope/schedule/budget

## Risks vs issues
- **Issue** = already happening; blocks a task until resolved (lack of understanding, access, skills…)
- **Risk** = may or may not happen; identified before the project starts; predictable from experience
- Both produce learning. Reference: Sankararajan & Shrivastava (2012), *Risks vs. Issues*, PM Network
- Risk register: risks from scope, team, clients, schedule, technology/tools, methodology → see [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Notes/Week 05 - Risk Management|Week 05 - Risk Management]]

## Processes (PMBOK)
- Identify stakeholders → Plan stakeholder management → Manage stakeholder engagement → (Monitor & control)
