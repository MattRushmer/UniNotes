# 12 - Resource Management

> Auto-extracted from [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Lectures/12 - Resource Management.pptx|12 - Resource Management.pptx]]
> Source text: *Information Technology Project Management* (Schwalbe, 2019), Chapter 9

## Importance
- People determine the success and failure of organizations and projects; managing human resources effectively is one of the toughest challenges for PMs
- Resource management = making the most effective use of the **human and physical resources** involved in a project
- IT workforce context: ICT spending ~$5.5T by 2020; IT jobs expected +12% by 2024; PM is #3 on Computerworld's hottest tech list

## Six resource management processes
1. **Plan resource management** (resource management plan + team charter)
2. **Estimate activity resources**
3. **Acquire resources**
4. **Develop the project team**
5. **Manage the project team**
6. **Control resources**

## Motivation theories
- **Intrinsic** (do it for enjoyment) vs **extrinsic** (reward/avoid penalty) motivation
- **Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs** - behavior guided by a sequence of needs
- **Herzberg's Motivational-Hygiene Theory** - motivators (achievement, recognition, work itself, responsibility, advancement, growth) vs hygiene factors (salary, supervision, work environment, benefits, training)
- **McClelland's Acquired-Needs Theory** - nAch (achievement), nAff (affiliation), nPow (power: personal vs institutional)
- **McGregor's Theory X** (workers avoid work -> coercion) . **Theory Y** (work is natural -> self-direction) . **Theory Z** (Ouchi, 1981 - Japanese approach: trust, quality, collective decision making)

## Influence and power
- **Thamhain & Wilemon** - nine ways to influence: authority, assignment, budget, promotion, money, penalty, work challenge, expertise, friendship
- Success is more likely with **work challenge** and **expertise**; failure more likely when relying on authority, money, or penalty
- **Power** = potential ability to influence behavior; types: coercive, legitimate, expert, reward, referent

## Effectiveness (Covey)
- Seven habits: be proactive . begin with the end in mind . put first things first . think win/win . seek first to understand, then be understood . synergize . sharpen the saw
- **Empathic listening** and **rapport** (mirroring) before communicating
- **Emotional intelligence (EI)** - knowing/managing one's own emotions and understanding others' (Gardner's multiple intelligences)

## Resource management plan and team charter
- Contents: project organizational charts . **responsibility assignment matrixes** (e.g. **RACI**: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) . staffing management plan and **resource histograms** . team charters

## Estimating and loading resources
- Tools: expert judgment, estimating approaches, data analysis, PM software, meetings
- **Resource loading** = amount of individual resources an existing schedule requires in specific time periods; a resource is **overallocated** when assigned more work than capacity
- **Resource leveling** = resolving resource conflicts by **delaying tasks** to create a smoother distribution; benefits: less management, just-in-time subcontractor use, fewer problems, better morale
- **Load balancing** (Kleinberg & Tardos, Algorithm Design) - greedy algorithm for minimizing **makespan** (optimal L* vs greedy approximation)

## Developing the project team
- **Tuckman model**: Forming -> Storming -> Norming -> Performing -> Adjourning
- Training and team-building activities
- **Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)** - 4 dimensions: E/I, S/N, T/F, J/P; NT types (rationals) attracted to technology
- **Social Styles Profile** - Drivers, Expressives, Analyticals, Amiables (assertiveness x responsiveness)
- Team-based reward and recognition systems

## Managing the project team
- Tools: interpersonal and team skills, PM information systems, conflict management
- **Conflict handling modes**: confrontation (problem-solving) . compromise . smoothing . forcing (win-lose) . withdrawal . collaborating
- Conflict can be good: new ideas, alternatives, motivation; **groupthink** = conformance without conflicting viewpoints
- **Task-related conflict** improves team performance; **emotional conflict** depresses it
- General advice: be patient, fix problems not blame, regular effective meetings, teams of 3-7, plan social activities, stress team identity, acknowledge accomplishments, support virtual team members

## Controlling resources
- Ensure physical resources are available as planned; monitor planned vs actual utilization and take corrective action
