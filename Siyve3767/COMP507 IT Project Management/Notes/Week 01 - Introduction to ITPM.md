# Week 01 - Introduction to ITPM (course overview)

> Auto-extracted from [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Lectures/Week 01 - Introduction to ITPM.pptx|Week 01 - Introduction to ITPM.pptx]] — Daniel Vaipulu

## Why ITPM matters
- IT PM knowledge is critical for IT graduates; industry is moving from specialists to **"T-skilled"** generalists
- The specialist PM role is disappearing, but PM tasks are embedded in other IT roles
- Teaching is **PMI-based** (Project Management Institute) → best practices from the **PMBOK Guide** + certification
- Leads into the R&D project courses (COMP702 & COMP703) and industry careers

## Course structure (per the deck)
- **10 knowledge areas + 5 process groups**, plus project phases and PM methodology
- Textbook: **Information Technology Project Management, 9th ed (Schwalbe, 2019)** — REQUIRED · **PMBOK Guide 6th ed** (AUT online)
- Weekly topics: W2 lifecycle/phases → W3 process groups & integration → W4 scope → W5 stakeholders → W6 schedule → W8 communications → W9 quality → W10 cost → W11 risk → W12 resource & procurement

## Assessments
- **Assignment 1 (50%, team)**: Part 1 — business case, stakeholder register & strategy, project charter (incl. team leader), team contract, kick-off/client/team meeting minutes, risk register v1, issue register v1, milestone report v1 · Part 2 — communications plan, change management plan, scope statement, **WBS**, **project schedule** (baseline, network diagram, critical path analysis), risk/issue registers v2, lessons-learned report
- **Assignment 2 (50%, individual)**: project cost control (EVM), schedule development, project selection, quantitative risk analysis
- Pass rule: ≥35% in each assessment AND ≥50% overall; expect 8–10 hrs/week

## PM documents/tools introduced (PMI templates)
- Business case · requirements document · scope statement · **WBS** · stakeholder register & strategy · project/network diagrams · communications plan · **quality assurance plan** · **budget plan / Earned Value Management (EVM)** · risk plan (qualitative & quantitative, register + calculations) · issue register
