# Northwest Airlines - ResNet Case Study (Project Initiation)

> Auto-extracted from [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Case Studies/Northwest Airlines - Case Study.pdf|Northwest Airlines - Case Study.pdf]] (Schwalbe, ITPM textbook, Chapter 12 - Initiating)

## Context
- 1992: **Fay Beauchine** becomes VP of Reservations at Northwest Airlines (NWA) - the reservations call center was a chronic money-loser
- New vision: focus on **sales, not just service** - measure number of sales, not number/length of calls; direct sales saved 13% on travel-agent commissions + 18% overhead
- A new information system was critical to the vision; **ResNet** would be managed by **business area leaders, not IS managers** - a first in NWA history (major culture change)
- 1993: **Peeter Kivestu** (marketing director) made project manager for the **ResNet Beta (prototype)** project; NWA nearly went bankrupt in 1993

## Project initiation concepts
- **Initiating** = recognizing and starting a new project or project phase; it is better to have moderate success on an important project than huge success on an unimportant one
- Selection of projects and of project managers is crucial
- Strategic planning is the foundation: the strategic plan (vision, mission, goals, objectives, strategies) drives which IT projects to pursue; IT is a **support function**, so it must serve business objectives (e.g. reducing costs)
- ResNet's primary objective: stop the financial drain of the reservation call centers

## Typical initiation outputs (Table 12-1)
- Knowledge area: **Scope** -> Process: **Initiation** -> Outputs: **Project Charter**, Project Manager identified/assigned, **Constraints**, **Assumptions**
- Tasks often involved: stakeholder analysis, feasibility study, initial requirements document
- Real projects often do not follow all textbook guidelines - groundwork is done before projects are official
