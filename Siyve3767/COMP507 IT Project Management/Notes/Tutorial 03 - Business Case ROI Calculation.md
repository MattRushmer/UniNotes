# Tutorial 03 - Business Case ROI Calculation

> Auto-extracted from [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Tutorials/Tutorial 03 - Business Case ROI Calculation.docx|Tutorial 03 - Business Case ROI Calculation.docx]]

## Part A - financial analysis (Excel)
For each year of a project, calculate:
1. **Discount factor** = 1 / (1 + rate)ᵗ (here discount rate = 7%)
2. **Discounted costs & benefits** = value × discount factor
3. **NPV** = Σ (discounted benefits − discounted costs)
4. **ROI** = (total discounted benefits − total discounted costs) / total discounted costs
5. **Payback period** = when cumulative (benefits − costs) turns positive
- Worked scenario: costs 150,000 (Y0) + 40,000 (Y1–3); benefits 200,000 (Y1–3)
