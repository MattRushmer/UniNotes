## 7.0 Budget Estimate and Financial Analysis

The project is capped at the allocated $200,000, spent in Year 0 (2025) to build and launch the Wiki. The main ongoing cost after that is internet access for the ~500 students using the two PMS platforms in ECMS labs — $6/student/month in 2025, rising 10% a year through 2027. Set against that is the $1,200,000/year software-cost saving ECMS expects once R&D teams are actually on the SaaS PMS instead of buying licenses upfront, realised from Year 1 (2026) once the Wiki is live and guiding tool selection.

We've treated the $1.2M saving as already net of the new $15/user SaaS fee, since that's how the brief phrases it ("reduce its software costs by...") — worth confirming with the lecturer before Part 2.

Using a 7% discount rate (per Tutorial 03), the project returns an **NPV of ~$2.80M** and an **ROI of ~809%** over the four-year analysis window, paying back the Year 0 cost within the first three months of Year 1. Full year-by-year workings are in Exhibit A.
