## Approach

Used the classic power/interest grid: plot each stakeholder on Interest x Influence, then pick one of four standard strategies per quadrant - **Manage Closely** (high/high), **Keep Satisfied** (low interest/high influence), **Keep Informed** (high interest/low influence), **Monitor** (low/low). This is the technique named but not detailed in [[COMP507 IT Project Management/Notes/Week 04 - Stakeholder Management|Week 04]], and it's what item 3 on the checklist is actually asking for.
